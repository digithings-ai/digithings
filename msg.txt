Code (scripts/mint_policy_check_token.py):
- ApiError separates "GitHub answered and said no" from every other failure, and
  main() now handles both halves symmetrically. A key that cannot mint, and a
  read that cannot complete, exit differently: FAILED (1) means the credential is
  proven wrong, SETUP_ERROR (2) means the probe could not tell. That distinction
  is what keeps a transport blip from being filed as a stale snapshot.
- Every request declares a 30s timeout.
- A redirect off api.github.com is refused rather than replaying the Authorization
  header at the new host.
- ttl is clamped to JWT_TTL_SECONDS, so no caller can widen the token lifetime.
