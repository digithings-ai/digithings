---
title: How it scales
order: 4
summary: >-
  Rented infrastructure scales with your invoice. Self-hosted infrastructure
  scales with your hardware and your judgment — a different shape entirely.
---

"Does it scale?" usually means one thing when asked of a rented service and a
different thing when asked of a stack you run yourself. A rented service
scales by raising a limit on someone else's dashboard, and the bill follows.
A self-hosted stack scales by you deciding what to add, when, and that
decision is yours to make badly or well.

This chapter is about that second shape: what grows, what does not have to,
and where the real ceiling sits once renting is no longer the constraint.
