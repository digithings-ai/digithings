---
title: "What a book is for here"
order: 1
summary: >-
  A book is not documentation.
---

A book is not documentation. Documentation is indexed by the thing you are
trying to do; a book is indexed by the thing you are trying to understand. The
same fact appears in both, but the book is allowed to spend a page on it.

Three things follow from that:

- **It is versioned as prose.** A chapter is a file with a header, not a row in
  a table that a migration can rewrite out from under you.
- **It is printable.** A chapter is read on a screen and on paper, from one
  file, so the diagrams are the same objects in both.
- **It has an order that the author chose.** The `order` field in the chapter
  header decides the sequence. Renaming a file does not move a chapter.
