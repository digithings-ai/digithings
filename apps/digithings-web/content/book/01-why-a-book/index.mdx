---
title: Why the book exists
order: 1
summary: >-
  The prose pages answer "how do I use this"; the book answers "why is it built
  this way", in the order that makes the answer readable.
---

The site has a docs section, and the docs section is good at its job: it tells
you the flags, the endpoints, and the order to type things in. What it does not
do is explain the shape. Every question of the form *why is it like this* ends
either unanswered or answered in a comment thread where the next reader cannot
find it.
