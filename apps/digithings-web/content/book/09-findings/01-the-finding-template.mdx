---
title: The finding template
order: 1
summary: >-
  Every finding states its verdict first, names its kind, and goes to the
  seat that owns the facts.
---

Every finding ticket follows the same shape, so a reader can tell in the
first screen what is claimed and what would settle it.

The title names the mismatch in one line. The body opens with the verdict:
what should be true, and what kind the finding is. **Drift** closes when the
document matches the tree again. **Decision** closes only after a ruling
picks which side wins, and says who gives it. Evidence comes after the
verdict, never before it: the board truncates long descriptions in list
views, so a template that buries its conclusion reads as a brief that stops
mid-sentence.

The ticket links the chapter section that surfaced it, and it is assigned
to the seat that owns the described surface. Four of the first five findings
were stale words in shared documents, and all four went to the owners of the
facts rather than the owners of the pages.

A finding that turns out to be false is withdrawn, not deleted, with the
reason it failed. The usual reason is a stale copy of the tree: the author
measured against a checkout that had fallen behind, and the document was
right all along. Recording that keeps the next author honest about checking
the current tree before filing.
