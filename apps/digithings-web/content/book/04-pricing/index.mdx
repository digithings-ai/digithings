---
title: Pricing
order: 4
summary: >-
  Self-hosting does not mean free, but it does mean the bill only ever has
  two honest lines on it: your own hardware, and the model usage you chose.
---

"What does it cost?" is the question every argument in this book eventually
has to answer, because an argument about ownership that ignores price is not
a serious one.

The honest answer for self-hosted AI infrastructure has two parts: the
software itself, and everything the software touches. They are priced
differently, and conflating them is how "free" ends up meaning something
misleading in either direction.
