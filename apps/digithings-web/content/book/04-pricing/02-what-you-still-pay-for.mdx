---
title: "What you still pay for"
order: 2
summary: >-
  Three honest costs remain: your own hardware, the model usage you choose
  to run, and, optionally, someone to help you integrate it.
---

Free software does not mean a free stack. Three costs remain, and none of
them are hidden:

- **Your own hardware.** Whatever you run digithings on — a laptop, a
  spare machine, a rented server — you pay for that the same way you would
  pay for it running anything else.
- **The model usage you choose.** Bring your own provider, and that
  provider bills you directly for what you use, at their price, under their
  terms. The stack forwards your request; it does not mark it up.
- **Integration work, if you want it.** Wiring the stack into an existing
  organization — data, identity, workflows that predate it — is real work,
  and paying someone to do it well is a legitimate cost. It is also the only
  line item digithings' maintainers charge for directly.

That third line is the business model, stated plainly: no hosted tier, no
markup on model usage, payment only for the work of fitting the stack to a
particular organization.
