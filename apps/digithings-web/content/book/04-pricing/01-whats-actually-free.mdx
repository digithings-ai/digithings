---
title: "What's actually free"
order: 1
summary: >-
  The software is free, in the specific sense that matters: no license fee,
  no seat count, no hosted tier to be upsold into.
---

digithings, as an example, is free in a specific and checkable sense: the
code is openly licensed, there is no license fee, no per-seat charge, and no
hosted tier waiting at the end of a trial to convert you. You can read every
line before you decide to run any of it.

That is a narrower claim than "costs nothing," and worth stating precisely
rather than generously. Free software still runs on hardware, and hardware
and electricity are never free — the claim is only that the software itself
asks nothing of you beyond that.
