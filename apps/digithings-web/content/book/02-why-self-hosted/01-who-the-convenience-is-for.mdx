---
title: "Who the convenience is for"
order: 1
summary: >-
  A rented platform is convenient for you and essential for its owner —
  and only one of those parties can change the terms.
---

A rented platform is built to be convenient, and it usually is: sign up,
paste in a card, start making requests. That convenience is not a gift. It is
the product, and its owner has every incentive to make leaving harder than
arriving.

The arrangement is not dishonest, just asymmetric. The platform's owner sets
the price, the limits, and the roadmap, and can revise any of them on a
timeline that suits their business rather than yours. You find out about a
change when it reaches your invoice or your rate limit, not before.

None of this makes renting a bad choice. It makes it a choice with a
direction: toward whoever holds the terms, not toward whoever is subject to
them.
