---
title: Why self-hosted
order: 2
summary: >-
  Rented infrastructure trades money for convenience every month. Owning it trades a slower start for a ceiling you control.
---

Every rented service is a bet that the convenience stays worth the price, and
that the terms do not change while you are not looking. AI infrastructure is
no different, except that the thing being rented — compute, a model endpoint,
a place to put your data — is unusually central to whatever you are building
on top of it.

The other option is to run the stack yourself. Not as a hobby or a protest,
but as a position with ordinary costs and an unusual upside.
