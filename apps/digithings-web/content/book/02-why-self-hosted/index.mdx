---
title: Why self-hosted
order: 2
summary: >-
  Renting infrastructure trades money for convenience, every month, for as
  long as you use it. Owning it trades a slower start for a ceiling that
  never arrives.
---

Every rented service is a bet that the convenience stays worth the price, and
that the terms do not change while you are not looking. AI infrastructure is
no different, except that the thing being rented — compute, a model endpoint,
a place to put your data — is unusually central to whatever you are building
on top of it.

This chapter is about the other option: running the stack yourself. Not as a
hobby, and not as a protest, but as a position with its own, ordinary costs
and its own, less ordinary upside.
