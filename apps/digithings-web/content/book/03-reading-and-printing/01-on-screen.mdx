A chapter page carries the table of contents for that chapter as a rail, and the
full book list as the previous/next pair at the foot of the page. Diagrams are
ordinary images until you ask for them to be bigger.

Dragging a diagram opens it: the image pans and zooms under the pointer, with
the wheel and with a pinch. It is an interaction on top of an image, so the
image is still there when the interaction is not — no script, no figure.
