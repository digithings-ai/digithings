---
title: Answers in shape, not in prose
order: 1
summary: >-
  How structured answers let services exchange results machines can rely on,
  and where that discipline pays off.
---

A model that answers in free prose leaves the caller parsing sentences.
A model that answers in a declared shape hands over data. The shared layer
makes the second the easy default: a caller passes a Pydantic model, digillm
asks the model for JSON matching its schema and validates the reply, so a
malformed answer is an error the caller can handle, not a string it has to
guess at.

The shapes stay small on purpose: a judgement with its reasons, a list with
its counts, a record with its fields. Small shapes are easier for a new model
to fill correctly, and services that need more compose small shapes rather
than inventing large ones.

> **Why digithings.** Structured answers over a format nearly every provider
> accepts mean results flow between services without translation layers, and
> a caller is not tied to the one vendor whose dialect it was written in.
