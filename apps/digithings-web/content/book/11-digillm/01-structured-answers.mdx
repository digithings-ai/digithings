---
title: Answers in shape, not in prose
order: 1
summary: >-
  How structured answers let services exchange results machines can rely on,
  and where that discipline pays off.
---

A model that answers in free prose leaves the caller parsing sentences.
A model that answers in a declared shape hands over data. The shared layer
makes the second the easy default: a caller declares the shape it needs,
the library enforces it, and the result arrives ready to store, display or
pass along.

The shapes stay small on purpose — a judgement with its reasons, a list
with its counts, a record with its fields. Small shapes survive model
changes; elaborate ones shatter on the first new vendor. Services that need
more compose small shapes rather than inventing large ones.
