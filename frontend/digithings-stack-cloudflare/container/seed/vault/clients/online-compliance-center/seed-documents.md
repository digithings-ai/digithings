# OCC documents and PDFs

The public help center publishes HTML FAQs and PDF guides. Full onboard will
ingest those into digisearch `occ_help` and this vault prefix.

Until crawl approval lands, this seed explains document expectations only — it
does not contain the binary PDFs themselves. Portal-only documents are out of
scope.
