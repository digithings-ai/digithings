# OCC getting started

Online Compliance Center (OCC) helps organizations manage compliance policies,
procedures, and help-center documentation.

## This assistant

digithings.ai/chat/occ retrieves from digisearch `occ_help` and vault notes under
`clients/online-compliance-center/`. Ask about policies, procedures, and public
help documents.

## Out of scope here

YouTube e-learning, the demo SPA, and the authenticated customer portal are not
in this corpus.
