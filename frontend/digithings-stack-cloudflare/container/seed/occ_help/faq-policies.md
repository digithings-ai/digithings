# OCC policies and procedures (seed)

## Policies

Policies are the authoritative rules an organization adopts for compliance.
In OCC help materials, policies are typically versioned documents that staff
must acknowledge or follow.

## Procedures

Procedures describe **how** to carry out a policy — step-by-step workflows,
roles, and checklists. Prefer citing the procedure document that matches the
user’s question rather than inventing steps.

## Versioning and review

- Keep policy documents versioned and reviewable
- Prefer official help-center articles over ad-hoc summaries
- When a newer revision exists, cite the current version if present in the corpus

## Answering policy questions

Ground answers in retrieved `occ_help` / vault hits. If the corpus has no matching
policy text, say so clearly instead of guessing regulatory requirements.
