## What is OCC?

Online Compliance Center (OCC) helps organizations manage compliance **policies**,
**procedures**, and **help-center documentation**. Public help content lives on
the Joomla help center; authenticated portals and demos are out of scope for this
corpus.

## How digithings chat helps

Ask about OCC policies, procedures, document types, and getting started. Answers
are grounded on digisearch index **`occ_help`** and vault notes under
**`clients/online-compliance-center/`**.
