canvas / review treepaper

Static review tree for the digiquant operator desk. Full bleed. Choosing a desk replaces the whole sidebar. house is a desk name: Brief, Portfolio, Pipeline, then Strategies, Tools and FX Hub, with Settings at the foot. Unbuilt items are tagged wip or soon. There is no House page. FX Hub is a first-class desk. rates watch stays its own desk. Figures are layout placeholders, not book data. Paper and research chips only.

01 / Shell

  • desk-picker.htmlThree desks in the picker. Choosing one replaces the whole sidebar. house is a desk name, not a page.

02 / Brief and charts

  • brief.htmlMorning scoreboard for the house desk. Hold on the paper book. Figures are layout placeholders.
  • ticker-dossier.htmlSPY deep link from a Holdings row. No extra spine item. Dossier tab named Profile is the name, not a House page.
  • gloomberg.htmlGloomberg pane on the brief. A labeled layout placeholder, not a live feed.
  • luxalgo.htmlLuxAlgo pane for SPY. A labeled layout placeholder, not a drawn series.

03 / Portfolio

  • holdings.htmlSticky tab. Weights, positions, and exposure on the house paper book.
  • theses.htmlSticky tab. Active cases, the evidence, and what would kill each.
  • tearsheet.htmlReport only. Performance, drawdown, exposures, holdings snapshot. No chart.
  • ledger.htmlSticky tab. Paper fills and cash. Timestamps are the paper broker clock.
  • attribution.htmlSticky tab. Sleeve and name contribution on the paper book since 2026-09-08.

04 / Pipeline and chat

  • pipeline.htmlRun health, node document, call trace, and artifact ledger on one screen.
  • chat.htmldigichat docked beside the run as a static transcript. Not an iframe.

05 / Settings

  • settings.htmlDesk+ controls for the house desk: paper brokers, keys, integrations, theme, prefs.
  • settings-paper.htmlPaper and research chips only. Live brokers are absent from this desk.

06 / FX Hub

  • fx-hub.htmlFirst-class desk. G10 board for the London read. Exposure on this desk is paper.
  • fx-ideas.htmlIdeas on the FX Hub spine. Synthesized research. No submit on this page.
  • fx-watch.htmlWatch on the FX Hub spine. Display-only levels. A research flag does not send.
  • fx-settings.htmlFX feed, generation stub, and paper venue. Account controls stay on shared settings.

07 / Rates watch

  • rates-digest.htmlrates watch stays its own desk. Daily read. This desk holds no paper book.
  • rates-watchlist.htmlNames this desk tracks. Research only. An unavailable mark is an em dash.

08 / Empty / loading / error

  • brief-empty.htmlHonest empty brief. Nothing estimated.
  • brief-loading.htmlBrief while the run is still writing. Figures withheld.
  • brief-error.htmlBrief after the run failed. Figures withheld.
  • holdings-empty.htmlHoldings when the paper book has no positions yet.
  • ledger-error.htmlLedger when the paper ledger did not load.
  • pipeline-loading.htmlPipeline while the run is still writing.
  • pipeline-error.htmlPipeline when analysts failed. The paper book is unchanged.
  • fx-empty.htmlFX Hub ideas when nothing was synthesized.
  • fx-loading.htmlFX Hub while the twelve-x research feed is still being read.
  • fx-error.htmlFX Hub when marks are unavailable and the feed returned no board.
