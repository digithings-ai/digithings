## Goal

Render `btc_sdca` in the strategy library and give it a tearsheet that describes what it actually
does. The taxonomy already has a `dca` kind in `components/tearsheet/strategy-kinds.ts` — nothing
renders for it yet, and the current tearsheet view would show a DCA book a long/short breakdown and a
meaningless win rate.

## Scope

### Library card (`strategy-library*.tsx`)

For `kind === "dca"`, swap the headline stat from win rate to **vs-lump-sum**, and show
capital-deployed rather than trade count. A card that reads "100% win rate, 412 trades" for an
accumulation book is worse than no card.

### Tearsheet view (`tearsheet-view.tsx`)

Suppress the long/short breakdown and the win-rate/profit-factor tiles when the schema reports them
null (drive off the null, not off a slug allowlist), and add:

1. **Valuation rails chart** — price with the `low`/`median`/`high` rails overlaid on a log scale.
   This is the single most explanatory chart for the strategy: it shows *why* it bought when it did.
   The rails come from the diagnostic columns #3168 emits.
2. **Risk band strip** — composite risk 0–100 over time, with the artifact's bands labelled: `<10
   Fire sale · 10–25 Accumulate · 25–50 Value · 50–75 Above mid · 75–95 Hot · 95–99 Bubble`.
3. **Accumulation view** — cost basis vs. spot price, plus capital deployed over time. Shows the
   averaging working (or not).
4. **Three-way benchmark** on the equity chart: SDCA vs. lump-sum vs. flat DCA.

### Current signal

For a DCA book, "current signal" is not long/short/flat — it is today's **risk score, band label,
and daily rate** ("Accumulate — buying 4.0% of cash today"). Extend `CurrentSignal` accordingly.
